Clean Time Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Clean Time does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, and the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores

Your counters (name, start date, optional daily cost), check-ins, urge logs, earlier stretches, and the names and phone numbers of people you add are stored in the app's private storage on your device. That storage is protected by your phone's own encryption. Clean Time opts out of Android backup, so none of it is copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app, or using Erase all my data in Settings, deletes it.

People and calls

When you pick someone from your contacts, Android's contact picker gives Clean Time only the name and number you choose. The app does not request permission to read your contacts. Call and text buttons hand the number to your phone's own dialer or messaging app; Clean Time never sees your calls or messages.

Notifications and widget

If you turn them on, Clean Time shows milestone and daily check-in notifications, scheduled on your device. Discreet mode keeps recovery and counter names out of notification text and the home-screen widget.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, Clean Time asks Android to confirm it's you with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. The app never receives that information; Android only tells it whether the check succeeded.

Exports (Pro)

If you export your log, you choose where the CSV file is saved using Android's file picker. Exported files are not encrypted, and what happens to them is up to you.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Clean Time requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS (only used if you turn on reminders), RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (to restore reminders after a restart), USE_BIOMETRIC (optional app lock) and com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play purchases). It does not request internet, contacts, phone, SMS or location permissions.

Not medical care

Clean Time is a personal tracking tool. It is not medical treatment or a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis in the United States, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.

Data we collect

None. Clean Time has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Clean Time opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Clean Time does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
