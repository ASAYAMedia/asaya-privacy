Evidence Locker Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Evidence Locker does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you record stays encrypted on your device, and the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything anywhere.

What the app stores, and how

Your records, entries, notes and files are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage, encrypted. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The keys are protected by your PIN and by your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore), so the data can't be read if the files are copied off the phone.

When you add a file, the app calculates its SHA-256 fingerprint from the original bytes and stores it with the file, so you can later show the file hasn't changed. Edits to entries are kept as numbered versions, and the app keeps an activity log of changes. Both stay on your device.

Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app, and the app hides its contents from the recent-apps preview.

There is no account, no cloud copy and no recovery. If you forget your PIN, nobody, including us, can open your records. If you turn on "Erase after 10 wrong PINs", the app destroys its keys and data after 10 wrong attempts. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Adding files

You can add files with Android's photo picker or file picker, take a photo with your camera app, or share files to Evidence Locker from other apps. Photos taken with the camera are written briefly to the app's private cache, then encrypted and the temporary copy is deleted. Files you add keep their original contents, including any metadata they already contain, because that information can matter as evidence.

Voice notes (Pro)

If you record a voice note, the app uses your microphone only while you are recording. The audio is encrypted as it is captured and is never written to storage unencrypted.

Exports

When you export a PDF timeline, an evidence bundle, or a copy of a file, you choose where it is saved using Android's file picker. Exported files are not encrypted. Where you store them and who you share them with is up to you.

Fingerprint unlock

If you turn it on, fingerprint or face unlock uses Android's own biometric system. The app never receives your biometric data; Android only tells it whether the check succeeded.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Evidence Locker requests: RECORD_AUDIO (only used for Pro voice notes, and only asked for the first time you record), USE_BIOMETRIC (optional fingerprint unlock) and com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play purchases). It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera or storage permissions. You can see the full list inside the app under Settings, Proof.

Not legal advice

Evidence Locker is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide legal advice, and whether any record is accepted as evidence is decided by the relevant court or authority.

Data we collect

None. Evidence Locker has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Evidence Locker opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Evidence Locker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
