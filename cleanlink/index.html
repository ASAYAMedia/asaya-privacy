Clean Link Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Clean Link does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It cannot: the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything off your device.

What the app does with your links

When you paste text into Clean Link, share text to it, select text and choose Clean links, or use the Clean clipboard tile or shortcut, the app reads that text on your device, removes tracking parameters and redirect wrappers from any links in it, and gives the result back to you. Everything happens locally using a rule list built into the app. Nothing is looked up online.

Clean Link keeps no history of the links you clean. It reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste from clipboard or use the tile or shortcut, and it writes to your clipboard only when you copy a result or use the tile or shortcut.

Your settings and any custom rules you add are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Clean Link requests only com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase. Android also adds an internal permission that stops other apps from sending the app private messages. You can see this list inside the app under Settings, Proof.

Data we collect

None. Clean Link has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Clean Link opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Clean Link does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
