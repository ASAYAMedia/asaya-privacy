Clean Shot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Clean Shot does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It cannot: the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send your images anywhere.

What the app does with your images

You choose images with Android's photo picker, or share them to Clean Shot from another app. Clean Shot never asks for access to your photo library. It reads each image on your device, uses text and face detection models built into the app to find things like email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, usernames and faces, and shows you what it found.

When you save or share, Clean Shot draws solid boxes over the areas you chose onto a brand-new copy of the image. That copy contains no location, camera or other metadata. Your original image is never changed.

Saved copies go to your device's Pictures/Clean Shot folder. Shared copies are written briefly to the app's private cache so the receiving app can read them, and are deleted the next time Clean Shot starts. What the receiving app does with the image is governed by its own privacy policy.

Your settings and any words you ask Clean Shot to always hide are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Clean Shot requests only com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase. It does not request access to your photos, camera, storage or location. You can see the full list inside the app under Settings, Proof.

Data we collect

None. Clean Shot has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Clean Shot opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Clean Shot does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
