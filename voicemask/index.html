Voice Mask Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Voice Mask does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It cannot: the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send your voice anywhere.

What the app does with your voice

When you record, Voice Mask captures audio from the microphone into the app's memory on your device. It does not write the recording to storage. When you import a voice note, you choose the file with Android's own file picker; Voice Mask reads it into memory and never changes the original.

The voice change is done on your device by code built into the app. Nothing is analysed or processed anywhere else. When you close a clip, or the app is closed, the recording and its masked version are gone.

Files you export

Voice Mask writes a file only when you choose Share or Save. Shared files are written briefly to the app's private cache so the receiving app can read them, and are deleted when you close the clip or next open the app. Saved files go where you choose. Exported files contain no name, location, device details or recording date: WAV files have no metadata at all, and the creation times inside M4A files are set to zero.

What a receiving app does with a file you share is governed by its own privacy policy.

A voice disguise changes how you sound. It does not hide what you say, and no disguise can be guaranteed to be impossible to undo. The app explains the strength of each mask so you can choose.

Settings

Your settings, such as your chosen mask and theme, are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never backed up or synced. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Voice Mask requests android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO, used only while the record screen is open, and com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase. It does not request access to the internet, your files, contacts or location. You can see the full list inside the app under Settings, Proof.

Data we collect

None. Voice Mask has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Voice Mask opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Voice Mask does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
