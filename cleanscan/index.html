Clean Scan Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer: ASAYA Media & Design LLC

The short version

Clean Scan does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It cannot: the app does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send what you scan anywhere.

What the app does with the camera and your images

While the scanner screen is open, Clean Scan passes camera frames to a barcode model built into the app. Each frame is read on your device and immediately discarded. Nothing is recorded or saved, and the camera is released as soon as you leave the scanner.

You can also pick photos with Android's photo picker, or share them to Clean Scan from another app. Clean Scan never asks for access to your photo library. It reads the chosen image in memory to find codes, then lets it go. It does not copy or keep your images.

What happens to a scanned code

The contents of a code are checked on your device against built-in rules, for example disguised addresses, lookalike website names, tracking tags and risky phone codes. No lookup is made on the internet.

Clean Scan never opens a code by itself. When you choose Open, Join network, Add to contacts, Add to calendar, Call, Write text or similar, the code's contents are handed to the app that does that job, such as your browser, Android's Wi-Fi settings, your contacts app or your calendar. You confirm the step in that app, and what it does is governed by its own privacy policy.

History

If history is switched on (it is by default), Clean Scan keeps the codes you scan in the app's private storage on your device so you can find them again. History is never backed up or synced, and is excluded from Android device transfers. You can clear it at any time, and switching history off deletes it. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Codes you make with the Make a code feature are created on your device. What you type there is not saved. Images you save go to your device's Pictures/Clean Scan folder; images you share are written briefly to the app's private cache so the receiving app can read them.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy; we never see your card, name or Google account. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation, which is stored on your device, encrypted, so Pro keeps working offline. Google Play's billing library normally includes a Google logging component. We remove it from the app when we build it, and the app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything to Google or anyone else.

Permissions

Clean Scan requests android.permission.CAMERA, used only on the scanner screen as described above, and com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets Google Play handle the Pro purchase. It does not request access to the internet, your contacts, calendar, location, messages, photos or Wi-Fi settings. You can see the full list inside the app under Settings, Proof.

Data we collect

None. Clean Scan has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers.

Backups

Clean Scan opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so nothing from the app is copied to your Google account.

Children

Clean Scan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
